Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Convoy of Bulls does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Our games are just games: no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Our games

This policy applies to all games published by Convoy of Bulls, including That Mountain Needs to Fall.

  • We do not collect any personal information from players.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Save files and settings are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us.
  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Third-party platforms

Our games are distributed through third-party stores such as Steam. When you buy, download or play a game through one of these platforms, the platform may process your data under its own privacy policy (for example, the Valve Privacy Policy). Convoy of Bulls has no control over, and receives no personal data from, those services beyond anonymous, aggregated sales figures.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, forms or analytics of our own. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security purposes, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our social channels listed on the home page.
